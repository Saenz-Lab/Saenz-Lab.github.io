We’re interested in people who like quantitative biology, molecular construction, and careful biophysics.

## How to reach out
Please email:
- a short note on what you want to work on
- a CV
- (optional) links to papers, code, or preprints

## Students
If you’re applying for an MSc/PhD rotation or thesis, include your timeframe and what skills you want to build.

## Postdocs
If you’re considering a fellowship, we’re happy to discuss fit and help shape a proposal.
