<div class="grid">
  <div class="card half">
    <h2>Design principles of living membranes</h2>
    <p>
      Membranes are not passive boundaries. Their lipid composition tunes biophysical properties that shape
      transport, energy coupling, and protein function. We map composition → physical state → phenotype.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="card half">
    <h2>Minimal cells as tuneable platforms</h2>
    <p>
      Genomically minimal bacteria offer a clean background to test how genetic and metabolic control
      sets membrane state. We use minimal cells as experimental platforms for membrane engineering.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="card half">
    <h2>Lipidome remodeling and homeostasis</h2>
    <p>
      Cells maintain membrane function across conditions by regulating lipid synthesis and remodeling.
      We refactor pathways and introduce synthetic control logic to probe and reprogram homeostasis.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="card half">
    <h2>RNA at membrane interfaces</h2>
    <p>
      RNA can interact selectively with lipid membranes, altering structure and activity.
      We investigate how membrane composition and physical state shape RNA function at interfaces.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
