# Sáenz Lab Academic Wiki

The Sáenz group studies how genomes encode the physical organization of living systems, with a focus on the cell membrane. Our research combines quantitative membrane biophysics, lipidomics, and synthetic genomics to understand how lipidomes are designed, adapted, and — increasingly — how they interact with RNA. This wiki compiles our lab's publications alongside earlier work and collaborations that built toward our current research program.

## Entities

- [[hopanoids|Hopanoids]] — pentacyclic isoprenoidal membrane lipids; bacterial "sterol surrogates"
- [[diplopterol|Diplopterol]] — the simplest hopanoid; orders membranes analogously to cholesterol
- [[bacteriohopanepolyols|Bacteriohopanepolyols (BHPs)]] — extended side-chain hopanoids; biomarkers and membrane lipids
- [[lipid-a|Lipid A]] — conserved core of LPS; partner of hopanoids in bacterial outer membrane ordering
- [[crocosphaera-watsonii|*Crocosphaera watsonii*]] — nitrogen-fixing marine cyanobacterium; early model for hopanoid biology
- [[methylobacterium-extorquens|*Methylobacterium extorquens*]] — gram-negative methylotroph; in vivo model for hopanoid membrane function
- [[mycoplasma-mycoides|*Mycoplasma mycoides*]] — simple pathogen; parent of JCVI-Syn3 minimal cells; tuneable membrane model
- [[jcvi-syn3|JCVI-Syn3A/B]] — genomically minimal cells; platform for lipidome design studies
